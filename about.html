About Us

History

Conquer The Belt Games is a fictional retail store, created in 2026, specializing in the sale of digital and physical games, accessories and products related to the fighting game universe. Although fictitious, its structure and operation reflect the model of a real store, with operations aimed at both the local public who love fighting games and online consumers. The company arose from the need to offer quality products at affordable prices to join the FGC (Fighting Games Community), in addition to centralizing information, releases and relevant content for players of different profiles. .

Our Mission

Make access to digital entertainment easier, faster and more accessible, with tips and suggestions.

Our Vision

To be recognized as the main fighting game store in the region, offering themed varieties, efficient service and an updated online experience.

Target Audience

Casual gamers, competitive gamers, parents, etc.
